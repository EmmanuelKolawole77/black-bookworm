    About us

    Black Bookworm started as a small shelf and a big belief: Nigerian readers should find Nigerian stories easily. We stock new releases and old favourites, and we put part of every sale back into the communities that read them.

    The Black Bookworm Foundation

    We donate books and run reading clubs in schools that have no library. Every purchase on this site helps fund a shelf.

    Learn more

    The Black Bookworm Prize

    An annual award for the best new book by a Nigerian author. The winner receives a cash prize and a national book tour.
